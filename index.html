What it does

  • Import everything. Manuscripts, notes, and art go in; a structured canon comes out.
  • Flags contradictions — every one cited back to its source, so you can judge for yourself.
  • Logs your rulings. When canon is disputed, your word is law, and it's on the record.
  • Tracks timelines and what each character knows at every point in the story.
  • Spoiler-safe outputs — share with collaborators without spilling the beans.

How it works

  • Bring your manuscripts, notes, and artwork.
  • Lorewarden builds your canon and watches for contradictions.
  • You rule on disputes; it keeps the timelines, the knowledge, and the secrets straight.

From manuscript to print

A production add-on carries approved manuscripts through illustration checks all the way to print-ready files — one continuous road from first draft to finished book.
